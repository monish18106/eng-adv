Welcome to Print & Packaging Engineering

Print and Packaging Engineering focuses on the design, production, and optimization of packaging materials and printed products. It integrates material science, mechanical processes, and sustainability principles.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on printing technology, packaging materials, and production processes.

  • B.Tech in Print & Packaging Engineering — Printing technology, packaging design, and production automation.
  • B.Sc in Packaging Technology — Materials science, packaging systems, and quality assurance.
  • B.Voc in Print & Packaging Management — Practical training in print operations, packaging, and process optimization.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in printing processes, packaging innovations, and sustainable materials.

  • M.Tech in Print & Packaging Engineering — Advanced printing and packaging technology, process optimization.
  • M.Sc in Packaging Science — Materials research, sustainable packaging solutions, and product design.
  • Ph.D. in Print & Packaging Engineering — Research in packaging materials, automation, and eco-friendly solutions.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Packaging Engineer

Designs and optimizes packaging materials and systems for products.

Print Technologist

Manages printing processes, quality control, and production efficiency.

Production Manager

Oversees print and packaging manufacturing operations and workflow.

Quality Assurance Specialist

Ensures printed products and packaging meet safety and quality standards.

Material Scientist

Researches and develops new materials for sustainable and innovative packaging solutions.
