Welcome to Robotics and Automation Engineering

Robotics and Automation Engineering focuses on designing, developing, and implementing robotic systems and automated processes. It combines mechanics, electronics, and computer science to build intelligent machines.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on robotics design, automation systems, and control engineering.

  • B.Tech in Robotics and Automation — Robotics programming, industrial automation, and mechatronics.
  • B.Sc in Robotics — Sensors, actuators, and embedded systems.
  • B.Voc in Automation Technology — Practical training in PLCs, industrial robots, and control systems.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in robotics, automation, AI, and intelligent control systems.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Robotics and Automation — Robot kinematics, AI for robotics, and automation system design.
  • M.Sc in Robotics — Robotics modeling, control theory, and embedded systems.
  • Ph.D. in Robotics and Automation — Research in AI-driven robots, autonomous systems, and industrial automation.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Robotics Engineer

Designs, builds, and tests robotic systems for industrial and commercial applications.

Automation Engineer

Develops automated processes and control systems to improve efficiency and safety.

Control Systems Engineer

Designs and maintains control systems for robotic and automated machinery.

Embedded Systems Engineer

Develops software and hardware for embedded robotic systems and smart devices.

AI Robotics Specialist

Integrates artificial intelligence into robots and automated systems for intelligent operations.
