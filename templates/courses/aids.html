Welcome to AI & Data Science

Artificial Intelligence and Data Science combine machine learning, statistics, and computing to derive actionable insights from large datasets. Applications include recommendation systems, predictive analytics, self-driving cars, and NLP solutions.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that equip students with core skills in AI algorithms, data modeling, and applied analytics.

  • B.Tech in AI & Data Science — Machine learning, neural networks, big data, and real-time analytics.
  • B.Sc. in Data Science — Statistics, data mining, programming, and visualization.
  • B.Voc in AI Applications — Practical exposure to AI tools, Python, and applied ML techniques.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Computer Science.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, CUET, or university-specific entrance tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in deep learning, AI research, and scalable data systems.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in AI & Data Science — Deep learning, AI ethics, large-scale data processing.
  • M.Sc. in Data Analytics — Business intelligence, statistical inference, real-world data modeling.
  • Ph.D. in AI / Data Science — Research in NLP, robotics, generative AI, and algorithmic optimization.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Data Scientist

Analyzes and interprets complex data to help organizations make better decisions.

Machine Learning Engineer

Builds predictive models and deploys AI systems using large datasets and algorithms.

AI Research Scientist

Conducts cutting-edge research in AI, deep learning, and robotics.

Data Analyst

Extracts insights from data through visualization, reporting, and statistical tools.

AI Consultant

Advises companies on AI strategy, tools, and technology adoption.
