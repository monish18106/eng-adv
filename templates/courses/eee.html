Welcome to Electrical & Electronics Engineering

EEE deals with the study and application of electricity, electronics, and electromagnetism. It covers a wide range of fields including power generation, electrical machines, control systems, and electronics.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses designed to build a solid foundation in electrical systems, circuits, machines, and power electronics.

  • B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering — Electrical machines, power systems, electronics, and control systems.
  • B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering — Basics of electromagnetism, circuits, and instrumentation.
  • B.Voc in Electrical Systems — Practical skills in power distribution, maintenance, and automation.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry or Electrical Technology.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced education in power electronics, renewable energy systems, and automation.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in EEE — Specializations in power systems, control engineering, or drives.
  • M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering — Focus on electrical machines, smart grids, and energy systems.
  • Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering — Research in high voltage engineering, power electronics, and energy management.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Electrical Engineer

Designs, tests, and supervises the development of electrical equipment and systems.

Power Systems Engineer

Focuses on generation, transmission, and distribution of electrical power.

Control Systems Engineer

Works on automation, control theory, and feedback systems for industrial processes.

Maintenance Engineer

Ensures electrical systems and equipment operate efficiently and safely.

Electrical Design Engineer

Creates schematics and layout for electrical systems in industries and buildings.
