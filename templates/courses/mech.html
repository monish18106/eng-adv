Welcome to Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering focuses on designing, analyzing, and manufacturing mechanical systems. It covers thermodynamics, robotics, fluid mechanics, and material science applications.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on core mechanical principles, manufacturing, and system design.

  • B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering — Mechanics, thermodynamics, design of machines, and manufacturing processes.
  • B.Sc. in Mechanical Science — Fundamentals of mechanics, materials, and energy systems.
  • B.Voc in Industrial Machinery — Practical training in mechanical systems, maintenance, and production techniques.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in mechanical design, robotics, energy systems, and manufacturing technologies.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Mechanical Engineering — Specializations in thermal engineering, CAD/CAM, robotics, or production engineering.
  • M.Sc. in Mechanical Systems — Focus on advanced mechanics, fluid dynamics, and material applications.
  • Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering — Research in advanced manufacturing, energy systems, automation, and robotics.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Mechanical Design Engineer

Designs mechanical components, systems, and machinery for various industries.

Production Engineer

Oversees manufacturing processes and optimizes production efficiency.

Automotive Engineer

Works on design, testing, and development of vehicles and automotive systems.

Robotics Engineer

Designs and develops robotic systems and automation solutions.

Thermal Engineer

Specializes in heat transfer, thermodynamics, and energy systems analysis.
