Welcome to Fashion

Fashion is the art of expressing creativity through clothing, accessories, and style. It combines design, aesthetics, and cultural influences to create trends and timeless looks.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs focusing on design, textiles, garment construction, and fashion marketing.

  • B.Des. in Fashion Design — Apparel design, pattern making, and fashion illustration.
  • B.Sc. in Fashion and Textiles — Textile science, fabric technology, and quality control.
  • BBA in Fashion Marketing — Brand management, retail strategies, and consumer behavior.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 from any recognized board; science or commerce stream preferred.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams or portfolio submission, depending on college.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in fashion design, textile innovation, and fashion business management.

  • M.Des. in Fashion Design — Advanced apparel design, sustainable fashion, and digital fashion.
  • M.Sc. in Textile Technology — Technical textiles, fiber science, and textile engineering.
  • MBA in Fashion Management — Leadership, global fashion markets, and merchandising.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Fashion Designer

Creates new clothing designs, selects fabrics, and develops collections for brands or clients.

Textile Designer

Designs fabric patterns, textures, and works with materials to enhance garment appeal.

Fashion Merchandiser

Plans product ranges, analyzes market trends, and manages inventory to maximize sales.

Stylist

Curates looks for photoshoots, events, or personal clients to reflect current trends and individual style.

Fashion Marketing Specialist

Promotes fashion brands through campaigns, social media, and strategic partnerships.
