Welcome to Geoinformatics

Geoinformatics involves collecting, analyzing, and visualizing geographic data to solve spatial problems using GIS, remote sensing, and GPS technologies.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on GIS, remote sensing, cartography, and spatial data analysis.

  • B.Tech in Geoinformatics Engineering — GIS applications, remote sensing, and spatial database management.
  • B.Sc. in Geography with Geoinformatics — Cartography, geospatial analysis, and environmental applications.
  • B.Voc in Geospatial Technology — Practical training in GPS, GIS software, and spatial data interpretation.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Geography.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in geospatial science, remote sensing technologies, and spatial data modeling.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Geoinformatics — Specializations in GIS software development, spatial analytics, and satellite data processing.
  • M.Sc. in Remote Sensing and GIS — Earth observation, environmental monitoring, and spatial decision support systems.
  • Ph.D. in Geoinformatics — Research in spatial data science, geospatial AI, and advanced cartography.

Job Roles & Descriptions

GIS Analyst

Analyzes spatial data and creates maps to support decision-making in various sectors.

Remote Sensing Specialist

Processes satellite imagery and aerial data for environmental and urban planning.

Cartographer

Designs and produces maps, integrating spatial data with graphical tools.

Geospatial Data Scientist

Applies data science and AI techniques to analyze complex geospatial datasets.

Surveying Engineer

Conducts land surveys and manages GPS data for construction and resource management.
