Welcome to Aeronautical Engineering

Aeronautical Engineering focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of aircraft and spacecraft. It combines aerodynamics, propulsion systems, structural analysis, and avionics to ensure safe and efficient flight.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that equip students with fundamentals of flight mechanics, aircraft design, and aerospace technology.

  • B.Tech in Aeronautical Engineering — Aerodynamics, propulsion systems, materials science, and aircraft design.
  • B.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering — Flight dynamics, aerostructures, and aviation systems.
  • B.Voc in Aircraft Maintenance — Practical training on aircraft systems, avionics, and safety protocols.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in aircraft design, propulsion, and aerospace research.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Aeronautical Engineering — Advanced aerodynamics, flight simulation, propulsion technologies.
  • M.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering — Research on aircraft systems, materials, and aviation safety.
  • Ph.D. in Aeronautics / Aerospace — Research in UAVs, space vehicles, and aerostructural optimization.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Aeronautical Engineer

Designs, develops, and tests aircraft and aerospace systems.

Aircraft Maintenance Engineer

Ensures proper functioning and safety of aircraft through maintenance and inspections.

Flight Test Engineer

Performs testing and validation of aircraft performance and systems.

Aerospace Research Scientist

Conducts research in aerodynamics, propulsion, and space technologies.

Avionics Engineer

Designs and maintains electronic systems used in aircraft and spacecraft.
