Welcome to Computer Science and Business Systems

Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) is an interdisciplinary program combining core computing technologies with business knowledge to develop professionals who can innovate and lead in the digital economy.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses that blend computing fundamentals with management, analytics, and business intelligence.

  • B.Tech in CSBS — Programming, data structures, cloud computing, and business analytics.
  • B.Sc. in Computer Science & Business — Combines core CS concepts with entrepreneurship, finance, and marketing.
  • B.Voc in Business IT — Practical skills in business applications, IT tools, and operations management.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Mathematics as a compulsory subject.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific exams.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Specialized courses that combine advanced computing with strategic business thinking and data science.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Data Science & Business Systems — Machine learning, AI, and business analytics.
  • M.Sc. in Information Systems — Business information systems, enterprise applications, and database management.
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science / Business Informatics — Research in decision support systems, digital transformation, and AI in business.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Business Analyst

Analyzes business data and trends to support strategic decisions and optimize processes.

Software Developer

Designs and builds software applications with business-oriented features.

Data Analyst

Interprets large datasets to uncover insights for business growth and operations.

IT Consultant

Advises companies on how to use technology to meet business goals efficiently.

Product Manager

Leads the development and lifecycle of technology products from a business perspective.
