Welcome to AI & Machine Learning

AI & Machine Learning focuses on designing algorithms and systems that learn from data to make predictions, automate tasks, and enable intelligent decision-making. It combines statistics, optimization, computer science, and domain knowledge to build practical AI solutions.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that provide foundations in mathematics, programming, and core machine learning concepts.

  • B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning — Machine learning, deep learning, data structures, and AI system design.
  • B.Sc. in Computer Science (AI specialisation) — Algorithms, probability & statistics, introductory AI, and practical ML projects.
  • B.Voc in Data Science & AI — Hands-on training in data pipelines, model development, and deployment.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Mathematics and preferably Physics/Computer Science.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, university-specific tests, or institute aptitude tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced programs for specialization in machine learning, AI research, and applied data science.

  • M.Tech / M.S. in Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning — Deep learning, reinforcement learning, advanced probabilistic models.
  • M.Sc. in Data Science / AI — Statistical learning, large-scale data processing, and research projects.
  • Ph.D. in AI / Machine Learning — Research in novel algorithms, interpretability, and domain-specific AI (NLP, CV, robotics).

Job Roles & Descriptions

Machine Learning Engineer

Implements and deploys ML models into production, optimizing performance and scalability.

Data Scientist

Extracts insights from data using statistical analysis and builds predictive models to solve business problems.

AI Researcher

Conducts research to develop new algorithms, publish papers, and push the boundaries of ML.

NLP Engineer

Designs and builds natural language processing systems for tasks like translation, summarization, and chatbots.

Computer Vision Engineer

Develops models and systems for image and video understanding, object detection, and visual recognition.

MLOps Engineer

Focuses on model lifecycle, CI/CD for ML, monitoring, reproducibility, and reliable deployment.
