Welcome to Leather Technology

Leather Technology focuses on processing raw hides and skins into finished leather products. It covers tanning, leather chemistry, quality control, and sustainable manufacturing techniques.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on leather processing, material science, and quality control.

  • B.Tech in Leather Technology — Leather processing, tanning, finishing, and product design.
  • B.Sc. in Leather Science — Leather chemistry, material testing, and production techniques.
  • B.Voc in Leather Manufacturing — Practical training in leather production, quality assurance, and industrial practices.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: University-specific tests or state-level CETs.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in leather engineering, product development, and sustainable leather technologies.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Leather Technology — Focuses on advanced tanning, finishing, and leather product design.
  • M.Sc. in Leather Science — Covers leather chemistry, quality assessment, and industrial research.
  • Ph.D. in Leather Technology — Research in innovative tanning methods, eco-friendly processes, and high-quality leather products.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Leather Technologist

Manages leather processing, tanning operations, and ensures quality of finished products.

Quality Control Officer

Inspects leather products for defects, durability, and standards compliance.

Leather Product Designer

Designs leather goods including footwear, bags, garments, and accessories.

Tannery Manager

Oversees tannery operations, chemical usage, and environmental safety.

Research Scientist (Leather)

Develops innovative tanning methods, sustainable processing techniques, and new leather materials.
