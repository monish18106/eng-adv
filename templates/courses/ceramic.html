Welcome to Ceramics Engineering

Ceramics Engineering focuses on the design, production, and application of ceramic materials, used in everything from electronics to aerospace. It integrates materials science, mechanical engineering, and chemistry to create innovative, durable products.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that teach students the fundamentals of ceramics science, materials technology, and manufacturing processes.

  • B.Tech in Ceramics Engineering — Study of ceramic materials, processing methods, and applications in various industries.
  • B.Sc. in Materials Science (Ceramics) — Focus on the properties and behavior of ceramics, including structure, processing, and performance.
  • B.Voc in Ceramic Product Technology — Practical training in ceramic product design, development, and production.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in ceramics material development, manufacturing processes, and industrial applications.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Ceramics Engineering — Advanced studies in ceramic materials, processing technologies, and manufacturing.
  • M.Sc. in Materials Science (Ceramics) — Research-based programs focused on ceramic material properties and innovations.
  • Ph.D. in Ceramics Engineering — Research in advanced ceramics, nanomaterials, and cutting-edge applications in industries like aerospace and electronics.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Ceramics Engineer

Designs, develops, and tests ceramic materials for various applications such as electronics, aerospace, and energy systems.

Materials Engineer

Focuses on the development and optimization of materials, including ceramics, to meet specific engineering requirements.

Process Engineer

Works on the manufacturing processes involved in ceramics production, ensuring efficiency, quality, and cost-effectiveness.

Research Scientist (Ceramics)

Conducts research on new ceramic materials, processes, and applications, focusing on improving performance and discovering new possibilities.

Quality Control Specialist

Ensures the quality and consistency of ceramic products through rigorous testing, analysis, and troubleshooting.
