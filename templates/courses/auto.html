Welcome to Automobile Engineering

Automobile Engineering focuses on the design, development, manufacturing, and testing of vehicles such as cars, trucks, and motorcycles. It integrates mechanical, electrical, and electronic engineering to produce efficient and safe vehicles.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that provide strong fundamentals in vehicle design, automotive systems, and production technology.

  • B.Tech in Automobile Engineering — Vehicle design, engine systems, and automotive materials.
  • B.E. in Mechanical (Automobile Engineering) — Thermodynamics, vehicle dynamics, and manufacturing processes.
  • B.Voc in Automotive Maintenance — Hands-on training in vehicle diagnostics, repair, and servicing.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on the institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or state-level engineering exams.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced programs focusing on automotive design, vehicle dynamics, and sustainable technologies.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Automobile Engineering — Advanced vehicle design, hybrid systems, and CAD/CAE analysis.
  • M.Tech in Automotive Engineering — Electric vehicles, autonomous driving systems, and advanced powertrains.
  • Ph.D. in Automotive Systems / Vehicle Engineering — Research in EV technology, vehicle safety, and intelligent transportation systems.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Automobile Engineer

Designs and develops vehicle components, systems, and prototypes.

Production Engineer

Oversees vehicle manufacturing processes to improve efficiency and quality.

Automotive Design Engineer

Focuses on the aesthetics and ergonomics of vehicles using CAD tools.

Vehicle Test Engineer

Tests performance, safety, and durability of automobiles before market release.

Research & Development Engineer

Works on new vehicle technologies such as electric, hybrid, and autonomous systems.
