Welcome to Pharmaceutical Engineering

Pharmaceutical Engineering focuses on the design, development, and production of medicines and drugs. It combines chemistry, biology, and engineering principles for safe and effective pharmaceuticals.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on drug formulation, pharmaceutical chemistry, and manufacturing technology.

  • B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy) — Drug design, pharmacology, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.
  • B.Tech in Pharmaceutical Engineering — Process engineering, quality control, and pharmaceutical production.
  • B.Sc in Pharmaceutical Science — Study of medicinal chemistry, pharmacognosy, and drug analysis.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: NEET, GPAT, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in drug development, pharmacology, and pharmaceutical technology.

  • M.Pharm (Master of Pharmacy) — Specializations in pharmaceutics, pharmacology, or pharmaceutical chemistry.
  • M.Tech in Pharmaceutical Engineering — Advanced pharmaceutical process engineering and quality assurance.
  • Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences — Research in drug delivery, formulation, and pharmaceutical innovations.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Pharmaceutical Engineer

Designs and oversees drug manufacturing processes and equipment.

Quality Control Analyst

Ensures the safety, quality, and compliance of drugs produced.

Formulation Scientist

Develops and tests new drug formulations and delivery systems.

Regulatory Affairs Specialist

Manages compliance with pharmaceutical regulations and approvals.

Research Scientist

Works on developing innovative medicines and improving drug efficacy.
