Welcome to Food Technology

Food Technology focuses on the science and technology behind food production, preservation, quality control, and innovation to ensure safe, nutritious, and sustainable food products.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on food processing, microbiology, food safety, and nutrition science.

  • B.Tech in Food Technology — Food processing, preservation, quality control, and packaging technologies.
  • B.Sc. in Food Science & Nutrition — Food chemistry, nutrition, and dietetics.
  • B.Voc in Food Processing — Practical training in food production, safety protocols, and quality assurance.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in food engineering, biotechnology, quality assurance, and food safety regulations.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Food Technology — Specializations in food processing, food safety, or product development.
  • M.Sc. in Food Science & Nutrition — Food biochemistry, functional foods, and nutritional research.
  • Ph.D. in Food Technology — Research in food microbiology, advanced preservation methods, and food innovation.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Food Technologist

Develops new food products, improves processing techniques, and ensures food safety standards.

Quality Control Analyst

Tests and monitors food quality, safety, and compliance with regulations.

Food Safety Officer

Implements safety protocols and audits food production processes to prevent contamination.

Product Development Specialist

Innovates and improves food products based on consumer trends and nutritional needs.

Nutrition Consultant

Provides advice on diet, nutrition, and healthy food choices to organizations and individuals.
