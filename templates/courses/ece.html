Welcome to Electronics & Communication Engineering

ECE focuses on the design, development, and testing of electronic systems and communication devices. It plays a vital role in fields like telecommunications, embedded systems, robotics, and VLSI design.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that provide core knowledge in electronics, circuits, signals, and communication technologies.

  • B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering — Analog and digital circuits, signal processing, communication systems.
  • B.Sc. in Electronics — Microprocessors, instrumentation, and circuit design.
  • B.Voc in Embedded Systems — Hands-on learning in microcontrollers, IoT, and system integration.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry or Electronics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced topics in communication systems, VLSI design, and wireless technologies.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in ECE — Specializations in communication, embedded systems, or signal processing.
  • M.Sc. in Electronics — Emphasis on analog/digital systems, instrumentation, and semiconductors.
  • Ph.D. in Electronics & Communication — Research in RF systems, photonics, and next-gen communication.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Electronics Engineer

Designs and develops electronic components, systems, and equipment.

Communication Engineer

Works on transmission, signal processing, and data communication systems.

Embedded Systems Engineer

Develops embedded hardware and software for automation and devices.

VLSI Design Engineer

Specializes in the design and testing of integrated circuits and chips.

Network Planning Engineer

Plans and optimizes communication networks for telecom and data services.
