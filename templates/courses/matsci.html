Welcome to Material Science Engineering

Material Science Engineering focuses on designing, analyzing, and developing materials used in technology and industry. It covers metals, polymers, ceramics, composites, and nanomaterials.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on materials properties, processing, and applications.

  • B.Tech in Material Science Engineering — Study of metals, polymers, ceramics, and composites; materials testing and characterization.
  • B.Sc. in Materials Science — Focus on material chemistry, physics of materials, and industrial applications.
  • B.Voc in Applied Materials Technology — Practical training in manufacturing, testing, and application of advanced materials.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in materials development, nanotechnology, and materials engineering applications.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Material Science Engineering — Focus on materials characterization, mechanical properties, and innovative materials.
  • M.Sc. in Materials Science — Covers material design, processing techniques, and applications in technology.
  • Ph.D. in Material Science Engineering — Research in advanced materials, nanomaterials, biomaterials, and industrial applications.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Materials Engineer

Develops and tests materials for use in industrial, aerospace, automotive, and electronics applications.

Metallurgical Engineer

Specializes in metals, alloys, and their processing for industrial applications.

Nanomaterials Researcher

Works on the development of nanomaterials for advanced technologies and applications.

Quality Control Engineer

Monitors and tests materials to ensure compliance with standards and specifications.

Process Development Engineer

Designs and improves processes for manufacturing materials efficiently and sustainably.
