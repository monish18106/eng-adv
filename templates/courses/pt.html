Welcome to Petroleum Engineering

Petroleum Engineering focuses on the exploration, extraction, and production of oil and gas resources. It combines geology, drilling, and reservoir management to efficiently recover energy resources.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on oil and gas exploration, drilling, and production techniques.

  • B.Tech in Petroleum Engineering — Drilling technology, reservoir engineering, and petroleum production systems.
  • B.Sc in Petroleum Technology — Petroleum chemistry, exploration techniques, and hydrocarbon processing.
  • B.Voc in Oil & Gas Technology — Practical training in field operations, drilling, and reservoir management.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in reservoir management, drilling technology, and petroleum production engineering.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Petroleum Engineering — Reservoir simulation, drilling optimization, and enhanced oil recovery.
  • M.Sc in Petroleum Technology — Hydrocarbon exploration, petroleum chemistry, and processing technologies.
  • Ph.D. in Petroleum Engineering — Research in unconventional resources, offshore drilling, and energy optimization.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Petroleum Engineer

Designs and manages methods for extracting oil and gas efficiently and safely.

Reservoir Engineer

Analyzes reservoirs to maximize hydrocarbon recovery and optimize production.

Drilling Engineer

Plans and supervises drilling operations for oil and gas wells.

Production Engineer

Monitors well performance and production systems to enhance output.

Petroleum Geologist

Studies subsurface formations to locate and evaluate oil and gas reserves.
