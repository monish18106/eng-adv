Welcome to Biotechnology Engineering

Biotechnology Engineering merges biology with technology to develop products and solutions that improve health, agriculture, and the environment. It focuses on genetics, molecular biology, bioprocessing, and bioinformatics to solve real-world problems.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that provide a foundation in biological sciences, chemistry, and engineering principles applied to living systems.

  • B.Tech in Biotechnology — Covers molecular biology, bioprocess engineering, genetics, and bioinformatics.
  • B.Sc. in Biotechnology — Focus on microbiology, genetics, biochemistry, and cell biology.
  • B.Voc in Bioprocess Technology — Practical training in fermentation, bioreactors, and quality control.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, NEET, or university-specific entrance tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced specialization in bioinformatics, genetic engineering, and biomedical research.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Biotechnology — Focus on genetic manipulation, bioprocess modeling, and enzyme engineering.
  • M.Sc. in Biotechnology — In-depth study of molecular genetics, immunology, and microbial technology.
  • Ph.D. in Biotechnology — Research in tissue engineering, genetic therapies, and biopharmaceuticals.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Biotechnologist

Develops products and technologies using living organisms or biological systems.

Genetic Engineer

Works on modifying genes to improve crops, develop therapies, or create new biological materials.

Biomedical Scientist

Conducts research on diseases and develops diagnostic and therapeutic solutions.

Bioinformatics Analyst

Applies computational tools to analyze biological data and genomic sequences.

Bioprocess Engineer

Designs and optimizes processes for large-scale production of biotech products.
