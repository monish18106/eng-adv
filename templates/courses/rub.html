Welcome to Rubber and Plastics Engineering

Rubber and Plastics Engineering focuses on designing, developing, and processing rubber and plastic materials for industrial and consumer applications. It covers polymer science, material testing, and manufacturing technologies.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on polymer science, rubber technology, and plastics manufacturing.

  • B.Tech in Rubber and Plastics Engineering — Polymer processing, material testing, and product design.
  • B.Sc in Polymer Science — Polymer chemistry, compounding, and rheology.
  • B.Voc in Rubber Technology — Practical training in rubber compounding, molding, and quality control.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in polymer engineering, material testing, and industrial applications.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Rubber and Plastics Engineering — Polymer processing, composite materials, and advanced manufacturing.
  • M.Sc in Polymer Science — Research in polymer synthesis, testing, and application development.
  • Ph.D. in Rubber and Plastics Engineering — Research in advanced polymers, biodegradable plastics, and material innovations.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Polymer Engineer

Develops and tests plastic and rubber materials for industrial and commercial applications.

Quality Control Engineer

Ensures material quality and performance standards in rubber and plastics manufacturing.

Product Development Engineer

Designs new rubber and plastic products and optimizes manufacturing processes.

Process Engineer

Manages production processes, including molding, extrusion, and compounding.

Research Scientist

Conducts R&D on new polymers, composites, and sustainable material solutions.
