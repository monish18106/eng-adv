Welcome to Environmental Engineering

Environmental Engineering focuses on protecting the environment by reducing waste and pollution. It involves designing sustainable systems for water treatment, air quality, and waste management.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on environmental systems, water resources, pollution control, and ecological engineering.

  • B.Tech in Environmental Engineering — Water and wastewater treatment, air pollution control, and solid waste management.
  • B.Sc. in Environmental Science — Environmental chemistry, ecosystem studies, and environmental monitoring.
  • B.Voc in Environmental Management — Practical training in waste management, pollution control, and sustainability practices.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Biology.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in environmental impact assessment, sustainability, and green technologies.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Environmental Engineering — Specializations in water resources, pollution control, or climate adaptation.
  • M.Sc. in Environmental Science — Environmental modeling, biodiversity, and sustainability science.
  • Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering — Research in advanced wastewater treatment, air quality engineering, and environmental policy.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Environmental Engineer

Designs and implements systems for pollution control, waste treatment, and environmental remediation.

Water Resources Engineer

Manages water treatment systems and sustainable use of water resources.

Air Quality Analyst

Monitors and analyzes air pollution levels, helping enforce environmental regulations.

Waste Management Engineer

Develops systems for solid and hazardous waste disposal, recycling, and composting.

Sustainability Consultant

Advises organizations on how to reduce their environmental impact through sustainable practices.
