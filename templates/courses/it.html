Welcome to Information Technology

Information Technology focuses on the use of computers, networks, and software to store, process, and transmit data. It plays a key role in digital transformation, automation, and cybersecurity across all industries.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on computer programming, data management, networking, and information systems.

  • B.Tech in Information Technology — Software development, networking, databases, and cybersecurity.
  • B.Sc. in Information Technology — Web development, operating systems, and data analytics.
  • BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) — Programming languages, cloud computing, and application design.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Computer Science.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state CETs, or institute-level IT entrance tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in software engineering, artificial intelligence, and information security.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Information Technology — Focuses on data science, networks, and advanced computing systems.
  • M.Sc. in Information Technology — Covers machine learning, cloud infrastructure, and big data analysis.
  • Ph.D. in Information Technology — Research in emerging technologies like AI, IoT, and quantum computing.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Software Developer

Designs, codes, and tests software applications across multiple platforms.

Network Administrator

Manages computer networks, ensuring connectivity, performance, and security.

Database Administrator

Oversees databases for reliability, performance, and data integrity.

Cybersecurity Analyst

Protects digital systems from cyber threats through risk assessment and monitoring.

Data Analyst

Analyzes and interprets large datasets to support decision-making and strategy.
