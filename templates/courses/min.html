Welcome to Mining Engineering

Mining Engineering involves the extraction, processing, and management of mineral resources. It focuses on safe, efficient, and environmentally responsible mining practices.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on mineral exploration, mining methods, and mineral processing.

  • B.Tech in Mining Engineering — Surface and underground mining techniques, mine planning, and safety.
  • B.Sc. in Mineral Resource Management — Study of minerals, geology, and resource evaluation.
  • B.Voc in Mining Technology — Practical training in drilling, blasting, and mineral processing.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in mining methods, mineral processing, and resource management.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Mining Engineering — Specializations in mineral exploration, mine safety, or extractive metallurgy.
  • M.Sc. in Mineral Technology — Study of advanced mineral processing and resource management.
  • Ph.D. in Mining Engineering — Research in sustainable mining, automation, and mineral extraction technologies.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Mining Engineer

Plans and manages extraction of minerals safely and efficiently.

Geologist

Studies rock formations and mineral deposits for mining projects.

Mineral Processing Engineer

Develops processes to extract and refine minerals from ores.

Mine Safety Officer

Ensures safety protocols and regulations are followed in mining operations.

Research Scientist

Works on new technologies for sustainable and efficient mining.
