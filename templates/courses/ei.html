Welcome to Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering

Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering focuses on the design and maintenance of instruments and control systems used in automation, manufacturing, and process industries. It blends electronics with measurement and control engineering.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs offering core knowledge in measurement systems, sensors, process control, and electronics.

  • B.Tech in Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering — Instrumentation systems, sensors, process control, and automation.
  • B.Sc. in Instrumentation — Focuses on analytical instrumentation, electronics, and calibration techniques.
  • B.Voc in Industrial Instrumentation — Practical training in control systems, PLCs, and field instrumentation.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry or Electronics/Instrumentation.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced specialization in control systems, industrial automation, and sensor technologies.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Instrumentation Engineering — Topics include advanced control, biomedical instrumentation, and robotics.
  • M.Sc. in Instrumentation — Emphasis on analytical instruments, transducers, and signal conditioning.
  • Ph.D. in Instrumentation & Control — Research in smart sensors, embedded systems, and industrial automation.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Instrumentation Engineer

Designs and manages systems for measuring and controlling process variables in industries.

Control Systems Engineer

Develops automation and feedback systems for efficient process control.

Process Control Engineer

Optimizes industrial processes through advanced control strategies and instrumentation.

Calibration Engineer

Ensures the accuracy and reliability of measurement instruments and sensors.

Automation Engineer

Implements PLC, SCADA, and DCS systems for industrial automation projects.
