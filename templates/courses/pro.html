Welcome to Production Engineering

Production Engineering focuses on planning, designing, and optimizing manufacturing processes to improve efficiency, quality, and cost-effectiveness in industrial operations.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Courses focused on manufacturing technology, process design, and industrial optimization.

  • B.Tech in Production Engineering — Manufacturing processes, industrial automation, and production planning.
  • B.Sc in Industrial Engineering — Process improvement, workflow analysis, and quality management.
  • B.Voc in Manufacturing & Production Management — Practical training in shop floor operations, production systems, and efficiency improvement.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced studies in production optimization, industrial management, and process engineering.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Production Engineering — Advanced manufacturing, automation, and process optimization.
  • M.Sc in Industrial Engineering — Production systems, lean manufacturing, and quality control.
  • Ph.D. in Production Engineering — Research in manufacturing technologies, industrial efficiency, and innovative production methods.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Production Engineer

Plans and supervises manufacturing processes, ensuring efficiency and quality.

Process Engineer

Optimizes industrial workflows and production techniques to reduce cost and waste.

Quality Control Engineer

Ensures products meet required standards and manufacturing compliance.

Industrial Manager

Oversees plant operations, resource allocation, and workforce management.

Maintenance Engineer

Maintains equipment and machinery to ensure continuous production efficiency.
