Welcome to Computer Science Engineering

Computer Science Engineering (CSE) focuses on the theoretical and practical foundations of computing, programming, algorithms, and system design. It equips students to develop software solutions and innovate in the digital age.

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

Programs that provide core computer science knowledge along with emerging technologies and practical skills.

  • B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering — Programming, algorithms, data structures, OS, and AI.
  • B.Sc. in Computer Science — Software development, databases, networking, and web technologies.
  • B.Voc in Software Development — Application development, mobile computing, and project-based learning.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • 10+2 with Mathematics and preferably Computer Science.
  • Minimum marks: 50–60% depending on institution.
  • Entrance exams: JEE Main, state-level CETs, or university-specific tests.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

Advanced programs in areas like AI, data science, cybersecurity, and system architecture.

  • M.Tech / M.E. in Computer Science and Engineering — Advanced computing, system design, and research.
  • M.Sc. in Computer Applications — Programming, analytics, and software design methodologies.
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science — Research in machine learning, networking, and software systems.

Job Roles & Descriptions

Software Engineer

Develops, tests, and maintains software applications and systems.

Data Scientist

Analyzes data to extract insights using algorithms, statistics, and machine learning.

Web Developer

Builds and maintains websites and web-based applications using front-end and back-end technologies.

Cybersecurity Analyst

Protects systems and networks from cyber threats and vulnerabilities.

Cloud Solutions Architect

Designs scalable and secure cloud-based IT infrastructures.
